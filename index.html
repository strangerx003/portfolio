About me

Engineering confidence into railway systems

Two years of hands-on RAMS and Safety Assurance work across rolling stock, signalling, telecom, power supply and OHE projects.

I'm a System Assurance / RAMS engineer supporting railway projects through the full EN 50126 lifecycle — from RAM planning and apportionment through analysis, demonstration and safety deliverables.

Day to day I develop and maintain RAM Plans, FMECA, RAM analysis & prediction and RAM demonstration reports, manage hazard logs through PHA/SHA/IHA studies, and run maintainability, spare-parts, Weibull and life-cycle-cost analyses to keep projects compliant and available.

I thrive working with multidisciplinary teams, suppliers, OEMs and stakeholders to turn reliability data into decisions.

  • Safety & assurance across the V-cycle
  • Data-driven reliability decisions

What I do

Core RAMS expertise

The disciplines I apply every day to make railway systems reliable, available, maintainable and safe.

RAM Analysis & Prediction

Apportionment, prediction and RBD modelling to verify contractual reliability and availability targets.

FMEA / FMECA

Subsystem failure mode, effects and criticality analysis driving design and maintenance decisions.

Hazard & Risk Assessment

PHA, SHA and IHA with structured risk evaluation and disciplined hazard log management.

RAM Demonstration

Demonstration plans and reports backed by FRACAS analysis of operational failure data.

Maintainability & Spares

MA, SMA, MTA and LORA studies plus spare-parts analysis to optimise availability.

Life Cycle Cost (LCC)

Preventive & corrective maintenance and spares cost modelling for asset decisions.

Weibull Analysis

Distribution fitting to characterise failure behaviour and optimise maintenance strategy.

Obsolescence Management

Obsolescence studies for rolling-stock subsystems — air duct, lighting, pantry and sanitary systems.
